## Screenshots Strategy
You need at least 4 good screenshots to show off the app on the Play Store. Now that the app is built, you can run it on your emulator or physical device and capture these moments:

1.  **Main Menu:** Take a screenshot of the sleek `RETRO PINBALL` title screen with the glowing neon green buttons.
2.  **Level Selection:** Highlight the dynamic difficulty options (Easy / Medium / Hard) to show players they can choose their challenge.
3.  **Mid-Game Action:** Capture a screenshot during gameplay! Make sure the ball is bouncing off a bumper (pink and blue glowing circles), and the flippers are raised.
4.  **High Scores:** Take a screenshot of the high scores screen after playing a few games to show the automatic DataStore tracking of the top 5 scores.
